# industry — индустриальные

## Case

Market review, regulation, ethics, security, competitors. The reader gets a
position and criteria, not a digest of press releases.

## When chosen

Several actors or approaches can be compared on one scale.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: охват or выбор. Reader: a decision maker scanning the field. One action:
inline CTA to a service or solution.

## Volume and structure

RU 900–1400 words. Structure: Comparison or Breakdown
(`structures/comparison.md`, `structures/breakdown.md`). The verdict-by-fit rule
lives in `structures/comparison.md`.

## Evidence

Named sources with dates. Market numbers with a period. The reader is handed a
decision by criteria.

## Links

Two or three services or solutions the comparison touches.

## Voice

Analytical, with a stated position. The text takes a side and defends it with a
scale, never with dismissal.

## Anti-examples

- «Обе стороны правы» with no verdict.
- A competitor named only to be put down.
- A market figure with no source or period.

## Spec asks

State `category: industry`, the criteria list, and the scale for each option.
