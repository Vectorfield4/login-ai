# media — медийные

## Case

Podcast, webinar, conference, AMA, digest. The recording becomes a standalone
text with a transcript, not a teaser.

## When chosen

There is a recording or a live event with substance worth transcribing.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: охват. Reader: someone who may skip the audio. One action: open a
related solution or breakdown.

## Volume and structure

RU 500–900 words. Structure: Breakdown (`structures/breakdown.md`). The page
carries the transcript and the click-to-load embed from `mediaUrl`.

## Evidence

Concrete takeaways with numbers or named examples. Timecodes are allowed. The
text is useful without listening.

## Links

The solution or service the recording serves.

## Voice

Conversational but edited: spoken language is cleaned, the point is kept.

## Anti-examples

- An announcement that ends where the substance starts.
- A transcript dumped with no structure.
- A recording the text cannot stand without.

## Spec asks

State `category: media`, the `mediaUrl` provider, and the transcript structure.
