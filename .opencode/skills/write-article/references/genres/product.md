# product — продуктовые новости

## Case

Release, update, new feature, API, price, availability. The reader learns what
the service can do and what the next step is.

## When chosen

A shipped change lands. The material sits next to the service page and links to
it.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: решение or выбор. Reader: an existing or evaluating client. One action:
open the service page or send a request.

## Volume and structure

RU 600–900 words. Structure: PSI or How-To (`structures/psi.md`,
`structures/how-to.md`). PSI for a shipped change, How-To for a setup the reader
repeats.

## Evidence

The feature's measured effect (time, cost, coverage) with conditions. A
release without a number becomes an announcement of a capability, not a result.

## Links

The service being updated, plus one adjacent solution. A case if the feature
already ran at a client.

## Voice

Concrete verbs and a plain description of the change. The version, API surface,
or price is named as is.

## Anti-examples

- A feature list with no effect.
- «Мы рады сообщить» and other self-praise.
- An upgrade with no migration note.

## Spec asks

State the `category: product`, an `excerpt` up to 300 characters, and the
AEO question «что умеет <услуга>», answered in the first sentence.
