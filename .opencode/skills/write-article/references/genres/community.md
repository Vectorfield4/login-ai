# community — сообщество

## Case

Open source, hackathon, contest, scholarship. The reader is invited and sees
the value of taking part through a result.

## When chosen

There is a call to participate with clear terms and dates.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: охват. Reader: a developer or specialist who might join. One action:
open a related solution or breakdown.

## Volume and structure

RU 500–800 words. Structure: PSI (`structures/psi.md`). The opportunity, the
terms, what the participant gets.

## Evidence

Terms, dates, and prizes stated as is. The link to product and practice is
spelled out.

## Links

The service or solution the program connects to.

## Voice

Inviting and plain. One action for the reader, no slogan.

## Anti-examples

- A call with no dates or terms.
- A contest framed as a marketing event.
- A prize list with no value to the participant.

## Spec asks

State `category: community`, the dates, and the single participant action.
