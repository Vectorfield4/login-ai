# corporate — корпоративные

## Case

Investments, partnerships, hiring, culture, ESG. The reader learns a fact about
the company and what it means for their work.

## When chosen

A company event has a client-side consequence worth stating.

## Reader and funnel

Stage: охват. Reader: a client, partner, or candidate. One action: open a
related solution or breakdown.

## Volume and structure

RU 600–900 words. Structure: PSI or Journey (`structures/psi.md`,
`structures/journey.md`). PSI for one event and its client-side effect; Journey
when the arc is the story.

## Evidence

Each claim gets a number or a dated event. The link to client value is stated
plainly.

## Links

The solution or service the event serves.

## Voice

Sober and factual. The company speaks about its work, not about its greatness.

## Anti-examples

- Self-praise with no fact.
- An investment announcement with no effect on the product.
- «Мы гордимся» as the whole paragraph.

## Spec asks

State `category: corporate`, the event date, and the client-side consequence in
one sentence.
