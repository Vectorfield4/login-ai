# Spec template

The spec is the working card for one article. Fill every section before writing
the body. Keep it next to the calendar row, not in the repo.

1. **Genre.** One of the eight, from `references/genres/`.
2. **Reader and funnel.** Stage (охват / выбор / решение), who reads, one
   target action.
3. **Query and AEO question.** Two or three target queries and one question the
   first sentence answers.
4. **Structure.** One of PSI / Breakdown / Comparison / How-To / Journey
   (`references/structures/`), with the section plan: headline, lead, three to
   six blocks with a job each, conclusion. The blocks are mutually exclusive and
   together cover the topic; three or four fit a reader's working memory.
5. **Headline and lead.** Three headline templates and two lead templates, each
   with a slot for a number.
6. **Evidence.** Required numbers, where they come from, and what to do without
   a measurement.
7. **Links.** Which services, solutions, cases, and adjacent breakdowns.
8. **Voice.** How the genre sounds and what to avoid.
9. **Anti-examples.** Three to five typical failures of the genre.
10. **RU/EN.** What translates literally and what adapts.
11. **Frontmatter.** `category`, `excerpt`, and `mediaUrl` values for the genre.
12. **Acceptance checklist.** Eight to twelve checks, verifiable without taste.
